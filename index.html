👁️ Appearance Guide

White: Lemon-green → Lemon → Gold → Amber → Brown

Red: Purple → Ruby → Garnet → Tawny → Brown

Rosé: Pink → Salmon → Orange

👃 How to Smell Wine

Smell first without swirling. Then swirl gently and smell again. Start broad: fruit, floral, herbal, spice, oak, earth. Only then become specific.

👅 Acidity

Acidity creates freshness and makes your mouth water. Pay attention to salivation after swallowing or spitting.

🍂 Tannin

Tannin creates a drying or gripping sensation on your gums, tongue and cheeks. It is especially important in red wine.

⚖️ Body

Body is the overall weight and texture of wine. Think of the difference between skim milk, whole milk and cream.

🍽️ Food Pairing

Acidic food generally needs an equally acidic wine. Sweet wine should generally be at least as sweet as the dessert. Salt can soften the perception of tannin. High alcohol can intensify spicy heat.

🎯 Training Rule

Taste first. Write your observations. Only afterward research the wine. Never let someone else's tasting notes tell you what you should smell.
