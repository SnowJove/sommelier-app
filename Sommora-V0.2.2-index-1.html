Wine Guide

Wine knowledge for every step of your journey.

👁️ Appearance Guide

White: Lemon-green → Lemon → Gold → Amber → Brown

Red: Purple → Ruby → Garnet → Tawny → Brown

Rosé: Pink → Salmon → Orange

💧 Understanding Acidity

Acidity creates freshness and makes your mouth water. Pay attention to salivation after swallowing or spitting. Higher mouth-watering sensation usually suggests higher acidity.

👃 How to Smell Wine

Smell first without swirling. Then swirl gently and smell again. Start broad: fruit, floral, herbal, spice, oak and earth. Only then become specific.

🍂 Tannin

Tannin creates a drying or gripping sensation on your gums, tongue and cheeks. It is particularly important in red wine.

⚖️ Body

Body describes the overall weight and texture of a wine. Think of the difference between skim milk, whole milk and cream.

🍽️ Food Pairing

Acidic food generally needs an equally acidic wine. Sweet wine should usually be at least as sweet as the dessert. Salt can soften the perception of tannin. High alcohol can intensify spicy heat.

🎯 Taste for Yourself

Taste first and record your observations. Research the wine afterward. Your own perception is the most important part of your journal.
